Breaking the Label Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 6, 2026  |  Last Updated: May 6, 2026

Breaking the Label ("we", "our", "the app") is a daily practice tracker for parents and educators supporting children with Individualized Education Programs (IEPs), developed by Frank Nowicki. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and how you can delete it. By using Breaking the Label you agree to the practices described below.

Summary: Breaking the Label collects nothing. The app requests no permissions, has no internet access, contains no analytics or advertising SDKs, and stores all of your data locally on your own device. Uninstalling the app deletes everything.

1. Information the App Accesses

Breaking the Label only stores the practice data you choose to enter inside the app. All of it is stored locally on your device in the app's private database — none of it is transmitted to us, to any server, or to any third party.

  • Child's first name (optional) — Used only to personalize the dashboard greeting on your device.
  • Grade level (optional) — Used only to display age-appropriate goals and prompts.
  • Daily goals — Reading-page, writing-minute, and math-minute targets you choose.
  • Reading sessions — Date, pages read, optional book title, optional notes.
  • Writing sessions — Date, minutes written, optional topic, optional prompt, optional notes.
  • Math sessions — Date, minutes practiced, subject, problems attempted, problems correct, optional notes.
  • Book chapter progress — Which chapters of the companion book you have marked as read.

What the App Does NOT Collect

  • No accounts, usernames, passwords, or email registration.
  • No analytics, crash reporting SDKs, or behavioral tracking.
  • No advertising identifiers or third-party advertising SDKs.
  • No location data of any kind.
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.
  • No internet access — the app contains no networking code.
  • No cloud sync — we do not operate any servers that could hold your data.

2. Permissions the App Requests

Breaking the Label requests zero Android permissions. The app's manifest declares no permissions of any kind. It cannot access the network, your contacts, your location, your storage, or your camera, because it does not ask to.

3. Third-Party Services

Breaking the Label uses no third-party services, SDKs, or analytics frameworks. The app is built with standard Android components (Jetpack Compose, Room database) and contains no integrations with Google, Meta, Amazon, or any other third party that would receive your data.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • All data is stored locally on your device using Android's private app storage and the Room database.
  • The app's database is sandboxed by Android — no other app on your device can read it.
  • Your data never leaves your device. The app has no networking code.
  • We operate no servers and have no ability to access, retrieve, or restore your data.

5. Data Retention

Breaking the Label retains your data only on your device, and only for as long as you keep the app installed and choose to keep the data:

  • Practice sessions, goals, and settings are retained until you delete them inside the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.
  • Book chapter progress is retained until you uncheck a chapter, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.
  • We never receive your data, so there is no server-side copy that could persist after you delete it locally.

6. How to Delete Your Data

You are always in full control of your data:

  1. Delete individual entries inside the app — Open Breaking the Label, navigate to the relevant tracker, and remove the entries you no longer want.
  2. Reset all app data — Open Android Settings → Apps → Breaking the Label → Storage → Clear Storage. This permanently removes the app database, settings, and all locally stored information.
  3. Uninstall the app — Uninstalling removes all Breaking the Label data from your device.

Because the app stores data only on your device and not on any server, performing any step above results in complete deletion. There is no separate request to make and nothing for us to delete on our end.

7. Children's Privacy

Breaking the Label is intended for use by parents, educators, tutors, and other adults tracking practice on behalf of a child. It is not directed to or marketed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app collects no personal information from any user — child or adult — and stores all data locally on the device, no child's information is ever transmitted to us or any third party. If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns about how the app is being used on a child's behalf, you can clear or uninstall the app at any time using Section 6 above.

8. Your Rights

Because all app data is stored locally on your device, you have direct, immediate access to it and full control over it:

  • Access: View all of your data inside the app.
  • Correct: Edit any setting or entry in the app.
  • Delete: Use any of the methods listed in Section 6.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be communicated through the app where reasonably possible.
